Privacy Policy

Version 1.1

Plain-language summary

We store your account details, the API key or credentials you provide for a connected fitness-tracking service (currently Liftosaur), and the workout data it syncs so we can compute your scores and run challenges. Your display name, lift performance, and scores are visible to other users through leaderboards and user pages. Your data is never sold, and there are no ads. Every page load requests fonts from Google Fonts (see Cookies & tracking); beyond that, outbound requests today go to your connected fitness-tracking service (using the credentials you provide), our mail relay when you request a password reset, our error-tracking and log-aggregation tooling (operated on infrastructure we control), and, if you sign in with SSO, our identity provider. We don't run analytics yet, but plan to add privacy-conscious, first-party usage analytics gated behind a consent banner and honoring the Global Privacy Control signal — see section 6 for the commitments that will govern it. Our servers run in Canada; our mail relay is in Switzerland, and Google Fonts may process data in the US — see International data transfers. The only email the Service sends is a password-reset link you asked for — no marketing emails.

This is a plain-language summary for convenience only. The numbered sections below are the actual policy.

1. Who we are

very lift ("we", "us", or "our") operates the very lift fitness-challenge web application. For the data described below, we are the party responsible for your information (the data controller).

2. Scope

This policy covers only the very lift service. It does not cover Liftosaur itself, which is an independent service with its own privacy policy, nor any other third-party service you may use.

3. Information we collect

The Service stores the following:

  • Account information — your username, an optional email address and display name, and your unit preference. The email address is optional: you can supply one when you sign up or add or remove it later in Settings, and it is used only to send you a password-reset link when you request one. An account with no email address on file cannot use password reset. For accounts created through single sign-on, an identifier linking you to our login provider is stored. The Service also records how your account was created — invite link, direct signup, single sign-on, or created by an admin — and, for a challenge you joined via a shareable invite link, which link admitted you. Today, single sign-on goes through an identity provider we control directly, not a third-party social account. If that is ever configured to let you sign in via an external social provider (e.g. Google or GitHub) instead, this policy will be updated to disclose what that provider shares with us before it's enabled.
  • Your fitness-tracking credentials — the API key or access credentials you provide for a connected fitness-tracking service (currently Liftosaur; we may support additional providers in future) are stored in the application's database so the server can fetch your data on your behalf. See Security for how this is handled.
  • Synced fitness data — your lift history (including the lift, weight, reps, dates, and equipment) fetched from your connected fitness-tracking service, along with logs of those sync operations.
  • Derived scoring data — the points and events the Service computes from your lifts.
  • Challenge participation — the challenges you are invited to or join, the goal chart you set for each one, and your join or leave status. If you build a goal chart from published strength standards, the Service also records alongside that chart the sex and the single bodyweight figure you entered at the time, because the suggested targets were calculated from them. That is a one-time record attached to that one goal: the Service does not track your bodyweight over time, does not keep a bodyweight history, and does not store a bodyweight for goals you set manually or from your lift history.
  • Server logs — routine technical logs generated when you use the Service, used for debugging and keeping the service running. These are forwarded to error-tracking and log-aggregation tooling we operate directly (see Sharing), not a third-party processor.

Sensitive information: the sex and bodyweight figure described above under Challenge participation is health-adjacent information. We don't treat it differently from your other fitness data, but we're calling it out explicitly rather than claiming we collect no sensitive information, since that wouldn't be accurate.

4. How we use it

Your information is used to run the Service: to compute your scores and tiers, to build leaderboards, to run challenge mechanics, and to send you in-app notifications. Notifications are shown inside the app only — the Service does not send marketing or notification emails. The one exception is a single transactional email, a password-reset link, sent only to the address on your account and only when you ask for a reset.

5. Visibility to other users

very lift is a shared, multi-user service. Your display name, lift performance, and scores are visible to other users through the leaderboards of the challenges you are in. Joining a challenge shares your results with the other participants of that challenge. Do not put information you want to keep private into your display name or other visible fields.

6. Cookies & tracking

Today: the Service uses only strictly-necessary cookies/storage, described in full below. It does not run analytics or advertising of any kind, and there is no cookie-consent banner yet because nothing beyond strictly-necessary cookies is set.

  • A session cookie to keep you signed in.
  • A CSRF cookie to protect form submissions.
  • Your light/dark theme preference, stored in your browser's local storage.

Every page also loads webfonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) directly from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), which discloses your IP address and browser characteristics to Google as part of that request. We plan to self-host these fonts to remove this dependency; until then, it is the one third-party request every page load makes.

Planned: we intend to add first-party usage analytics (e.g. page and feature engagement, session frequency, challenge lifecycle counts) to understand how the Service is used. When we do, the following commitments will govern it, and this section will be updated with the specifics:

  • A consent banner will gate collection; declining disables tracking while the Service remains fully usable.
  • Data stays first-party — no analytics data will be sent to or processed by a third party.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers; there is no advertising on the Service.
  • Analytics infrastructure runs on infrastructure we control directly.
  • The Service will honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal: a browser sending it is treated as an automatic opt-out, with tracking skipped entirely and no consent banner shown.

There's no uniform standard for the older "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser header, so like most services, we don't respond to it. Global Privacy Control (above) is its practical, better-supported successor.

7. Sharing

Your data is not sold. There are no advertisements. The Service makes outbound requests to Google Fonts on every page load (see Cookies & tracking), and to your connected fitness-tracking service (using the credentials you provide, to fetch your data), our mail relay when you request a password reset, and, if you sign in with single sign-on, our identity provider (login server). Sending a password-reset email discloses your email address and username to that mail relay. Server logs — which may include technical details like your IP address for security purposes such as rate-limiting — are forwarded to error-tracking and log-aggregation tooling that we operate directly on infrastructure we control; this is not a third-party processor, and that data is not shared onward. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

Business transfers: if we ever sell, merge, or transfer the Service to another party, your information may transfer as part of that, but it would remain governed by a privacy policy at least as protective as this one, and you'd be notified before it took effect.

8. International data transfers

The Service's servers run in Canada, as does the error-tracking and log-aggregation infrastructure we operate directly (see Sharing) — it introduces no new jurisdiction. Some requests the Service makes on your behalf do leave Canada: our mail relay (Migadu) is based in Switzerland, and Google Fonts (see Cookies & tracking) is served from Google's global infrastructure, likely including the United States. Wherever you access the Service from, your information may be processed in these locations as described above.

9. Retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to deactivate or delete your account and associated data. Deletion applies to what is stored in the live application; we are not able to guarantee removal from any external service you have separately connected, such as Liftosaur. The sex and bodyweight figure recorded with a strength-standards goal chart (see Information we collect) is kept for as long as that goal chart exists, since it is the record of how those targets were calculated; it is removed when your account and its data are deleted.

10. Your rights

You can view and correct much of your account information directly in Settings. For access to, export of, or deletion of your data beyond what Settings offers, contact us.

11. Security

Passwords are stored using Django's password hashing and are never stored in plain text. The Service is served over HTTPS. Be aware that your fitness-tracking credentials are stored so the server can act on your behalf to fetch your data; treat them as sensitive and rotate them with that provider if you believe they have been exposed.

12. Minors

The Service is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18 — see the Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected in the version and dates shown above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or requests concerning your data can be directed to us at admin@verylift.com.
