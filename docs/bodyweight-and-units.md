# Units, bodyweight & added-weight lifts

Your unit preference and how weighted bodyweight exercises like pull-ups are
shown and scored both matter for reading your numbers correctly. This page
covers both, plus the one bodyweight figure the app keeps.

## Choosing kg or lb

You can set your preferred unit — kilograms or pounds — in your account
settings. This only changes how weights are **displayed** to you; behind
the scenes everything is tracked consistently, and workouts pulled from
your tracker are automatically converted to match, so you always see numbers
in the unit you're comfortable with.

## How added-weight lifts are shown

For exercises like the **chin-up, pull-up, and dip**, what matters to you
isn't your total bodyweight lifted — it's how much extra weight you're
adding (or how much assistance you're using). So these are shown relative
to your bodyweight rather than as a raw total:

- **BW** — bodyweight only, no extra weight added.
- **+5** — 5 units of extra weight added, shown in your preferred unit.
- **-10** — band- or machine-assisted, effectively subtracting from your
  bodyweight.

Every other lift is shown as the plain weight you lifted, in your preferred
unit.

## Your bodyweight

You can store one current bodyweight, either during signup or from your
account settings. Where your connected tracker records weigh-ins, we read the
most recent one and keep your figure up to date automatically.

It has exactly one job: suggesting targets for chin-ups, pull-ups and dips.
Because a target for those is an *added* weight, a suggestion can't be
calculated without knowing what you already carry, and the goal-setup
Compute button uses the same figure for the same reason. It is never used to
score a set, and never shown to anyone else.

Only the one current number is kept, never a history of it, and it is
entirely optional. Skip it and you'll simply be asked once, in the moment, if
a challenge with a bodyweight-added lift comes up.

## How added-weight lifts are scored

For chin-ups, pull-ups, and dips your goal chart's target is itself an
**added weight**, not a total load — so scoring compares the added weight
you actually lifted directly against that target, with no bodyweight
arithmetic involved. A logged set of `+5` beats a `+3` target; `-10`
(band-assisted) beats a `-15` target.

The one exception: sets logged on assisted-machine equipment (e.g. a
Leverage Machine) record your **net total load**, not added weight, so
there's no way to compare them against an added-weight target. Those sets
never score on chin-ups, pull-ups, or dips — log them as free or
band-assisted reps instead if you want them to count.
