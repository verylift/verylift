# Welcome to very lift

very lift turns your workouts into a friendly challenge. Connect
your lift log, join a challenge with friends, and watch your points climb as
you hit new strength milestones — no spreadsheets, no manual entry.

Here's a quick map of the guides below. Jump to whichever answers what you're
wondering about right now.

## A quick overview

When you join a challenge you build your own goal chart — pull published
strength standards, suggest targets from your recent lift history, or set
your own numbers from scratch. Every lift you complete is then compared
against that chart. Hit a target and you earn a point — heavier singles are
worth more, lighter higher-rep sets are worth less, and the app always tells
you the fastest way to close the gap to your next point.

Nothing to set up beyond connecting your account and joining a challenge —
the scoring, syncing, and leaderboard updates all happen automatically in the
background.
